Dr. Hasibullah Hakimi

Head of Student Affairs

Hasibullah Hakimi is a fifth-year medical student from Afghanistan with a strong passion for medical research, medical education, and academic development. He is actively involved in scientific research, teaches medical subjects, and is committed to promoting evidence-based learning among medical students.

Hasibullah is an active member of Mind i Research and Capacity Building Center, where he contributes to research, student development, and academic initiatives. His professional interests include clinical medicine, public health, and advancing healthcare through research, education, and collaboration. He is dedicated to continuous learning and making meaningful contributions to medicine and healthcare in Afghanistan and beyond.

Research helps expand scientific understanding, improves academic quality, and supports evidence-based decision-making — playing a key role in identifying challenges and developing effective solutions, especially in medicine and education.
