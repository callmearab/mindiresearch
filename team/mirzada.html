Dr. Maryam Mirzada

Executive Director

Dr. Maryam Mirzada is a Front-End Developer, Technology Instructor, and a Computer Science and Medical Sciences student from Afghanistan. She is passionate about web development, technology education, research, and empowering women through digital innovation. Her research and professional interests include front-end development, digital literacy, entrepreneurship, public health, and the application of technology to education and community development.

Maryam is an Aspire Institute Alumna and has experience leading educational, technology, and community-based initiatives. She is committed to developing innovative digital solutions and advancing education, research, and sustainable social impact.

Afghanistan is among the countries where many tasks are still carried out without research. In every field of knowledge, research should be the foundation of all work.
