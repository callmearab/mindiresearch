Dr. Abdul Rahim Soroush

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Dr. Abdul Rahim Soroush is the Founder and CEO of Mind i Research and Empowering Center, an initiative dedicated to advancing research capacity, scientific collaboration, and academic excellence. He is a medical doctor with a strong passion for evidence-based medicine, research methodology, and systematic reviews and meta-analyses.

Dr. Soroush has authored and co-authored scientific publications in peer-reviewed international journals and is actively involved in medical research and academic collaboration. His research has been recognised internationally, with abstracts accepted and presented at leading scientific conferences, including CHEST, UEG, AHA, TCT, and several other prestigious international congresses.

He is committed to empowering students, researchers, and healthcare professionals through research training, mentorship, scientific writing, and international collaboration. Through Mind i, Dr. Soroush aims to inspire the next generation of researchers, promote high-quality scientific publications, and contribute to improving healthcare by fostering innovation, critical thinking, and evidence-based practice, particularly in resource-limited settings.

Due to the challenges within Afghanistan’s academic society, I established Mind i to bring scientific transformation, publish academic papers, produce knowledge, and strengthen the country’s academic community.
