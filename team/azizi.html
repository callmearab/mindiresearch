Dr. Abdulbasit Azizi

HR Officer

Dr. Abdulbasit Azizi is a third-year medical student at Kabul University of Medical Sciences (KUMS), Afghanistan, with a strong interest in academic medicine, clinical research, and evidence-based healthcare. His research interests focus on general surgery, cardiovascular medicine, and the application of artificial intelligence in healthcare.

He has developed experience in systematic reviews and meta-analyses and has been actively involved in collaborative medical research. He also serves as a peer reviewer for international medical journals, contributing to the advancement of high-quality scientific research.

One of Mind i’s primary goals is not only to conduct research, but to teach research methodology to the new generation of academics — nurturing talent and empowering people to reach their full potential.
