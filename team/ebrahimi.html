Dr. Masouda Ebrahimi

Vice President for Academic Affairs

Dr. Masouda Ebrahimi is a fourth-year medical student at Shokan Ualikhanov Kokshetau University, Kazakhstan. She is passionate about clinical research, evidence-based medicine, and systematic reviews and meta-analyses. Her research interests include medical education, public health, and the application of artificial intelligence in healthcare.

Masouda is an Aspire Leadership Program alumna and an OMLAS Fellow. She is also the founder of the Morning Team Initiative, through which she supports students by sharing educational and scholarship opportunities. She is committed to advancing collaborative research and contributing to impactful scientific discoveries.

Research is the backbone of scientific progress, transforming ideas into evidence-based knowledge and guiding accurate decision-making in education and healthcare. Without research, knowledge remains incomplete.
