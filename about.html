01 — About the center

A home for research culture in Afghanistan

Mind i was established as a scientific, non-profit institution to promote a culture of research and knowledge production among students and young people. We focus on education, mentorship, and hands-on scientific research across medicine, engineering, economics, computer science, and other disciplines — guiding students from their first research question all the way to a published academic paper, under the supervision of experienced mentors.

Medicine· Engineering· Economics· Computer Science· + more

Marginal note — our ultimate goal

  • ✓ Transform students into real, capable researchers — not just students who study research.
  • ✓ Improve the quantity and quality of scientific research produced inside Afghanistan.
  • ✓ Contribute to knowledge production and problem-solving through evidence, not assumption.
