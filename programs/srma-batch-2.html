Second batch: research methodology and systematic review and meta-analysis

Dates
April 1–30, 2026
Place
Mazar-e-Sharif, Afghanistan
Venue
iRokhsh Learning Center
Photographs
19

We are proud to announce the successful completion of the second batch of our intensive training program on Research Methodology and Systematic Review & Meta-Analysis (SRMA).

This comprehensive course was conducted from April 1 to April 30, 2026, designed to take participants from foundational concepts to a professional level in research practice. The program emphasized hands-on learning, including formulating research questions (PICO), literature searching, screening, data extraction, analysis, manuscript preparation, and submission of manuscripts to journals.

The training was independently organized and delivered by Mind i Research Center, and hosted at iRokhsh Learning Center. The participants demonstrated remarkable dedication, discipline, and active engagement throughout the course.

We are delighted to recognize all participants for their commitment and successful completion of the program. We believe the knowledge and skills gained will significantly contribute to their future scientific and medical careers.

We remain committed to empowering the next generation of Afghan researchers through quality education and evidence-based practice.
