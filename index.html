01 — About the center

A home for research culture in Afghanistan

Mind i was established as a scientific, non-profit institution to promote a culture of research and knowledge production among students and young people — guiding them from their first research question all the way to a published academic paper, under the supervision of experienced mentors.

04 — Contributors

Meet the people building Mind i

Dr. Abdul Rahim Soroush

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Due to the challenges within Afghanistan’s academic society, I established Mind i to bring scientific transformation, publish academic papers, produce knowledge, and strengthen the country’s academic community.

Dr. Maryam Mirzada

Executive Director

Afghanistan is among the countries where many tasks are still carried out without research. In every field of knowledge, research should be the foundation of all work.

Dr. Masouda Ebrahimi

Vice President for Academic Affairs

Research is the backbone of scientific progress, transforming ideas into evidence-based knowledge and guiding accurate decision-making in education and healthcare. Without research, knowledge remains incomplete.

Dr. Abdulbasit Azizi

HR Officer

One of Mind i’s primary goals is not only to conduct research, but to teach research methodology to the new generation of academics — nurturing talent and empowering people to reach their full potential.
