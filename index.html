Mind i was established to promote a culture of research and knowledge production among students and young people.

Mind i was established as a scientific, non-profit institution to promote a culture of research and knowledge production among students and young people. Under the supervision of experienced mentors, we guide them from their first research question all the way to a published academic paper.

Read more about Mind i

From a first question to a published paper

A structured mentor–mentee system carries each student through every step of the route.

  1. First question

    Pick a question worth studying, shaped with a mentor from the start.

  2. Research skills

    Learn to frame a question, search the literature, and analyse data.

  3. A real study

    Carry out a genuine research project inside a multidisciplinary team.

  4. Published paper

    Prepare the manuscript, submit it to a journal, and get support for a first paper.
